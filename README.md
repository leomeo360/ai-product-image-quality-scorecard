# AI Product Image Quality Scorecard

A free, practical 100-point review checklist for AI-assisted ecommerce product images. Use it before publishing a generated or edited product photo. The score is a review aid, not proof that an image is accurate or legally usable.

## Quick start

1. Put the original product photo, approved packaging/artwork, and product facts beside the candidate image.
2. Check the hard stops below first. If any apply, reject the image regardless of its score.
3. For each criterion, award full points if it passes, half points if a visible defect is repairable without changing approved product facts, or zero if it fails.
4. Record the reviewer, date, defects, source permissions, and final decision. Recheck the final exported file, not just the editor preview.

| Section | Points | What to check |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Product fidelity | 30 | Silhouette and geometry (8); dimensions and scale (6); components and controls (6); label and logo placement (5); brand color and material (5) |
| Physical realism | 25 | Contact shadow (5); light direction (5); reflections (5); material response (5); perspective and surface contact (5) |
| Composition and delivery | 20 | Crop and safe area (5); visual hierarchy (5); copy space (5); edges and output quality (5) |
| Factual integrity | 15 | Text and marks (5); claims and performance (5); props and context (5) |
| Rights and review record | 10 | Source permission (4); human review (3); decision record (3) |

## Hard stops

Reject the image even if its total score is high when:

- A protected logo, label, certification, legal line, or price is invented or altered.
- Product geometry suggests a feature the real product does not have.
- The scene makes an unsupported performance, ingredient, safety, or endorsement claim.
- Permission for the source image or included brand material cannot be established.
- Crop, blur, compression, or decoration hides a known defect in the delivered file.

## Example review

For a skincare bottle, compare the cap shape, bottle proportions, label text, and color with approved references. Then inspect contact shadow and reflections at full resolution. A polished background cannot compensate for a fabricated ingredient claim or altered label: those are hard stops. Record a fix request such as “restore the approved label artwork and soften the contact shadow,” then review the new export again.
